Our Story

A Legacy of
Royal Taste

Noor Mahal was born from a passion for preserving the authentic culinary heritage of the Mughal era. For over two decades, we have served dishes crafted from secret family recipes passed down through generations.

Every spice is hand-selected, every dish prepared with devotion. We believe that great food is not just nourishment — it is an emotion, a memory, a moment of joy.
